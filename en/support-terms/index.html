4. Refund conditions

4.1. General principle

Voluntary support is a monetary gift under Article 572 of the Civil Code of the Russian Federation.

If you voluntarily select an amount, confirm the transaction, and make a gift, changing your mind afterward does not, by itself, entitle you to a refund.

For example, if you voluntarily give RUB 500 (five hundred Russian rubles), then decide the next day that RUB 100 (one hundred Russian rubles) would have been enough, that change of mind alone does not create a right to recover the difference.

4.2. When you may request a review

4.2.1. Technical duplication of a payment

You may request a review if one payment transaction initiated by you is processed more than once because of a technical error, although you did not initiate additional transactions.

For example, you initiate one RUB 200 (two hundred Russian ruble) payment but two identical amounts are charged.

Each new payment independently initiated by you is considered a separate contribution.

For example, 5 (five) separately initiated payments of RUB 3,000 (three thousand Russian rubles) each are not automatically technical duplicates, even if you later decide that you wanted to give less.

The Recipient reviews the Website’s transaction records and information available from Robokassa. If duplicate debiting occurred within Robokassa or a bank without creating a separate Website record, the Recipient may investigate with the relevant provider.

4.2.2. Discrepancy in the payment amount

You may request a review if the amount actually debited differs from the amount generated by the Website for the relevant transaction.

For example, the Website generates a payment for RUB 500 (five hundred Russian rubles), but your account is debited RUB 1,500 (one thousand five hundred Russian rubles).

The Recipient compares the Website’s transaction records with Robokassa records and may request further clarification from the payment service or bank.

Differences resulting from currency conversion or charges imposed by the Donor’s bank are examined separately and do not necessarily mean that the Recipient received more than the displayed amount.

4.2.3. Mistake when selecting an amount

If you accidentally selected a different amount from the amount you originally intended to give, you may contact the Recipient and explain the circumstances.

For example, you intended to contribute RUB 100 (one hundred Russian rubles), but selected and confirmed RUB 1,000 (one thousand Russian rubles).

Your request will be reviewed individually.

The Recipient may consider available records of the amount selected and the transaction completed. A statement that you clicked the wrong amount or later changed your mind does not, by itself, require a full or partial refund.

Where the law provides grounds for invalidating a transaction, the relevant legal provisions apply.

4.2.4. Payment made without the payment instrument holder’s consent

If you discover a transaction that you did not make or authorize, contact your card-issuing bank or payment provider immediately to dispute it. You may also notify the Recipient.

The Recipient may examine the available transaction records and contact Robokassa.

The Recipient has no access to SMS codes, biometric authentication results, or other internal bank authentication records.

4.2.5. Other grounds provided by law

A refund, invalidation of a transaction, or revocation of a gift may also be possible where the relevant legal requirements are met, including under:

  • Article 178 of the Russian Civil Code: material mistake;
  • Article 179: fraud, coercion, threats, and other circumstances covered by that Article;
