Introduction

This book is not a scientific work or a textbook. Everything I write about is my personal opinion, based solely on my own life experience and on what I have read and thought through. It does not replace a consultation with a doctor, a psychologist or any other specialist. If you need help, please reach out to one.

This book will tell you how life works from the soul’s point of view. Or, if you like, on behalf of the soul. What tasks the soul has for this lifetime. What level of development your soul has reached at this point in your life. The book will tell you how to understand and help yourself, how to understand and help your children and others close to you.

You have the right to believe everything said here—or not. Each of us has the right to choose. Always. Even if you feel that you are in a hopeless situation, remember: you always have the right to choose, and there are always several ways to resolve your problem or situation.

Each of you will find something in this book that is close to your spirit—something that will resonate inside you. And before you set this book aside, or any part of it, close your eyes, try to clear your mind of all other thoughts, and ask yourself: “Is there truth in this book?” And you will hear the answer. If you hear “Yes,” then start reading, and find the very truth you are ready for at this moment. You don’t have to be ready to take in everything written here right away. You may want to reread this book so that your mind can take in and accept a little more of what it says.

In this book I will describe my sense of the world and my worldview, which rest on my personal observations, on the information I have eagerly sought throughout this lifetime, and on my analysis of that information. I have come a long way in separating the truth out of various sources—the truth that agreed with my own conclusions and with my sense of this world. And by the word “world” I mean not just this planet, but everything that exists, regardless of whether we, as human beings, are aware of it or not.

The book follows one overall idea: I will move from the particular to the general, from “myself” to the wider circle around us. And we will limit ourselves to our close circle: children, family and other people close to us. And believe me, that is really no small thing. If each of us walks this path of understanding ourselves and our inner circle, then everything around us will inevitably change for the better.

And again, you have the right to accept my conclusions or not. For my part, I will give you hints that you can use. And you will decide for yourself whether you need to use them.

I deliberately chose not to send everything I wrote here to a publisher’s proofreader. Let my observations about life and my conclusions stay as they are, with all their imperfections. I want you to understand that each of us has the right to make mistakes, me included. That goes for mistakes in life, too—though it depends on how you look at it: were they really mistakes? There is no point in feeling sorry for yourself or reproaching yourself for the past; it cannot be changed. You can only correct the present, by drawing the right conclusions from your past. Never forget that who you are now is the result of your past. Always. So it does not matter whether it was good or bad. It is thanks to your past that you are here and now. And it is the analysis of your past and the awareness of your present that will move you forward in your development. The events around you and, importantly, the state of your soul will tell you whether you have chosen the right direction for yourself, or whether it needs adjusting.
