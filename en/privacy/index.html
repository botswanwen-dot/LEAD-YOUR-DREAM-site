2. Purposes, categories and legal grounds for processing personal data

2.1. Conclusion and performance of contracts

For the purposes of placing orders, making payments, providing purchased products, restoring access to electronic editions, handling requests and performing contractual obligations, the Operator may process:

  • the buyer’s email address;
  • first name and surname, if provided;
  • the order number, date, time and amount of payment;
  • information about the purchased product;
  • the payment transaction identifier;
  • information about the version of the Public Offer accepted;
  • information about the generation of links, the provision and downloading of files;
  • information about access restoration;
  • information about requests, technical problems and refunds.

Legal grounds for processing: the conclusion and performance of a contract with the data subject, the fulfilment of obligations established by law, and other applicable grounds provided for by Article 6 of Federal Law No. 152-FZ.

Email address of a buyer of an electronic edition. When an order for an electronic edition is placed, the Operator receives the email address provided by the Buyer and processes it for the purposes of concluding and performing the contract, transmitting the necessary information to the Robokassa payment service to generate and send the electronic fiscal receipt, providing links to the electronic files, restoring access to the purchased edition and handling the Buyer’s requests.

The email address is processed on the basis of clause 5, part 1, Article 6 of Federal Law No. 152-FZ without separate consent, since the processing is necessary for the conclusion and performance of the contract.

If payment is not completed, the email address is deleted from temporary storage no later than 24 hours after the order is created, unless there are other legal grounds for keeping it.

After successful payment, the email address is stored in plain form for the time needed to send the message with links to the electronic edition and to complete the planned delivery retries. The plain address is then deleted from the operational order database.

To automatically verify the right to restore access within the period established by the contract, a protected verification value of the email address may be kept, which allows it to be matched against an address re-entered by the Buyer without storing the original address in that database.

Information about the order, payment, provision of the electronic edition and performance of obligations is kept for the periods established by the legislation of the Russian Federation and this Policy.

The email address is not used for advertising mailings without a separate legal basis.

If the sale of physical goods is introduced, the data processed may additionally include the information required for delivery, including the recipient’s name, delivery address and contact details. The corresponding provisions apply from the moment delivery is actually introduced and the necessary documents are updated.

2.2. Handling requests

When a request is sent to the Operator, the following may be processed:

  • the sender’s email address;
  • the sender’s name, if provided;
  • the content of the request;
  • information about the order or a voluntary support payment, if the request relates to the relevant transaction;
  • other information voluntarily provided by the sender.

The data is used to consider the request, provide a response, perform contractual obligations, settle claims and protect the legitimate interests of the parties.

Legal grounds: performance of a contract, fulfilment of obligations established by law, or the consent of the data subject where applicable.

2.3. Website traffic analysis

The Yandex Metrica web analytics service is used to analyse traffic, assess how users interact with the Website pages, and improve the Website’s content, usability and performance.

Depending on the settings of the service, the following may be processed:

  • IP address;
  • cookies and technical identifiers;
  • information about the device type, browser and operating system;
  • information about the pages visited;
  • the date, time and duration of the visit;
  • information about user actions on the Website pages, including clicks on interface elements;
  • approximate information about geographic location.

Personal data is processed for the purposes of optional web analytics on the basis of the visitor’s consent where such consent is required by law.

2.4. Limitation of the data processed

The Operator does not purposefully collect special categories of personal data or biometric personal data of visitors and buyers for the purpose of sales through the Website.

The processing of personal data is limited to the extent necessary to achieve the relevant purposes.

2.5. Receipt of voluntary financial support for the author

When the “Support the author” function is used, the Operator processes the personal data of individuals to the extent necessary to arrange, record and confirm the voluntary gratuitous transfer of funds, to consider requests and to refund erroneous payments where there are legal grounds.

Depending on the technical implementation, the following may be processed:

  • the payer’s email address, if it is transmitted to the Operator;
  • the payment transaction identifier;
  • the date, amount and status of the transfer;
  • information about the payer’s requests;
  • information needed to check a disputed transaction and to arrange a refund through the original payment service.

The legal grounds for processing are the conclusion and performance of the relevant civil-law transaction, the fulfilment of obligations established by law and other applicable grounds under Article 6 of Federal Law No. 152-FZ.

The personal data of persons who have provided voluntary support is not used for advertising mailings without the necessary consent.

Full bank card details of payers are not collected or stored by the Operator.

2.6. Receipt, moderation and publication of reviews

For the purposes of obtaining feedback on the author’s products, pre-moderating and publishing reviews, reading private messages from visitors and handling requests to remove reviews, the Operator may process:

  • the name or pseudonym provided by the visitor;
  • the category of the author’s product;
  • the stated place of purchase;
  • an optional rating;
  • the review text;
  • the content of the optional private message to the author;
  • the date the review was received, the record identifier and moderation information;
  • information voluntarily provided in a request to remove a review.

Registration, an email address and a telephone number are not required to submit a review.

Reviews are published only after moderation. The public display name is masked. Before publication, the Operator takes steps to remove or replace information that could identify individuals, without distorting the meaning of the review.

Private messages submitted through the field “Would you like to share something?” are not published and are not made available to other visitors to the Website. They are intended solely for the Operator. No individual response to such messages is provided.

The legal grounds for processing are determined in accordance with Article 6 of Federal Law No. 152-FZ, including the consent of the data subject where applicable and other grounds provided by law.

Where it becomes necessary to disseminate the personal data of individuals, separate consent and the other requirements of Article 10.1 of Federal Law No. 152-FZ apply.

Retention periods for this information are set out in Section 6 of this Policy.

3. Procedure and methods of processing personal data

3.1. The Operator may perform the following actions with personal data: collection, recording, systematisation, accumulation, storage, clarification, retrieval, use, transfer in cases provided for by law, blocking, deletion and destruction.

3.2. Personal data may be processed by automated and non-automated means, as well as by mixed processing.

3.3. The Operator ensures that the volume of personal data processed corresponds to the stated purposes of processing.

3.4. When paying through a payment service, the Operator does not receive or store the full details of the bank cards of buyers and of persons providing voluntary support. The data required directly for making a payment is processed by the payment organisation as part of the services it provides.

3.5. Personal data obtained when placing an order or making a voluntary support payment is not used for advertising mailings without the necessary legal basis and the prior consent of the data subject in cases provided for by law.

3.6. Personal data may be transferred to authorised persons and organisations to the extent necessary to achieve the purposes of processing, in compliance with the legislation of the Russian Federation.

4.1. To carry out certain operations, the Operator uses the following services to the extent of the functionality actually in use:

  • Robokassa — for accepting payments, making settlements and refunds;
  • Yandex Cloud — for storing order information, reviews and private messages from visitors, technical support for the delivery of electronic products, the generation of links, the organisation of review moderation and related processes;
  • Yandex Metrica — for analysing Website traffic and visitors’ interaction with its pages.

4.2. Personal data is transferred to the above organisations to the extent necessary for the relevant purposes, where there are legal grounds. If an organisation processes personal data on the Operator’s instructions, the relationship is formalised in compliance with the requirements of the law.

4.3. As of the date of approval of this Policy, the Operator does not carry out cross-border transfers of personal data.

4.4. If the need for a cross-border transfer of personal data arises in the future, the Operator will assess the legal grounds and conditions for such a transfer and, before it begins, will fulfil the requirements of the legislation of the Russian Federation, including sending the relevant notification to the competent authority in the cases established by law.

4.5. The Operator does not sell the personal data of buyers and visitors to third parties.

4.6. If the services used, the places of storage or the methods of processing personal data change, the Operator will take the necessary measures to update this Policy and other information required by law.

5. Cookies and web analytics technologies

5.1. The Website may use cookies, technical identifiers and similar technologies necessary for its operation, as well as web analytics tools.

5.2. Optional analytics technologies that require consent are activated only after it has been obtained.

5.3. Visitors are given the opportunity to decline optional analytics. Declining it does not prevent the use of the Website’s main functions or the purchase of the author’s products.

5.4. Visitors may change their decision on the use of optional analytics technologies via the “Cookie settings” link on the Website.

5.5. Disabling optional analytics does not exclude the use of technical mechanisms objectively necessary for the operation of the Website, where there are legal grounds for their use.

5.6. The visitor’s choice regarding the use of Yandex Metrica is stored in their browser and includes the decision made, the date and time of the choice and the version of the Policy. Consent is requested again after 12 (twelve) months or when the version of the Policy changes.

5.7. Yandex Metrica is used on all language versions of the Website subject to the visitor’s prior consent. Session Replay (Webvisor) and form analytics are disabled. To analyse the usability of the Website, the click map, link click tracking and traffic indicators are used.

5.8. If a visitor declines Yandex Metrica, the analytics counter is not loaded. If consent is withdrawn, the counter stops working and Metrica’s technical data in the browser is deleted to the extent technically possible for the Website.

6. Periods of processing and storage of personal data

6.1. Personal data is processed and stored for no longer than the purposes of processing require, unless other periods are established by the legislation of the Russian Federation.

6.2. A download link for a purchased electronic product is valid for 24 (twenty-four) hours from the moment it is generated, unless another period is provided for by the terms of purchase of the relevant product. Automatic access restoration is available within the period established by the relevant Public Offer.

6.3. The expiry of a download link or of the automatic access restoration period does not mean that the processing and storage of all personal data related to the order ends.

6.4. Information about orders, payments, the provision of electronic products, voluntary support for the author and the performance of contractual obligations is stored for the periods necessary to comply with the legislation of the Russian Federation, keep tax records, consider claims and protect the legitimate rights and interests of the Operator.

6.5. Once the purposes of processing have been achieved, personal data is subject to deletion or destruction, unless there are other legal grounds for its further storage.

6.6. If consent to the processing of personal data is withdrawn, the Operator stops the corresponding processing, unless there are other legal grounds for continuing it, and ensures the destruction of the data within the periods established by the legislation of the Russian Federation.

6.7. Retention of reviews and private messages

Published reviews are retained for up to three years from the date of publication, with the possibility of extending retention after assessing its necessity and lawfulness.

Rejected reviews are deleted within 30 calendar days after the moderation decision, unless there are other legal grounds for retention.

Private messages from visitors submitted through the field “Would you like to share something?” are automatically deleted no later than 90 calendar days after receipt, unless the law requires otherwise.

Requests to remove reviews and related information are processed and retained to the extent and for the periods necessary to handle the request and to fulfil obligations established by law.

7. Measures to protect personal data

7.1. The Operator takes the necessary legal, organisational and technical measures to protect personal data against unlawful or accidental access, destruction, alteration, blocking, copying, provision, dissemination and other unlawful actions.

7.2. Depending on the nature of the processing and the information systems used, protection measures include:

  • restriction and differentiation of access rights;
  • protection of accounts and means of authentication;
  • the use of secure connections;
  • control of access to information systems;
  • backup where necessary;
  • control of storage periods and data deletion procedures;
  • detection and investigation of events related to personal data security breaches.

7.3. Access to personal data is granted only to persons who need it to perform the relevant functions and who have legal grounds to process it.

7.4. The Operator applies protection measures in accordance with Articles 18.1 and 19 of Federal Law No. 152-FZ and other applicable legal requirements.

8. Rights of data subjects

8.1. The data subject has the right to:

  • receive the information about the processing of their personal data provided for by law;
  • demand the clarification, blocking or destruction of personal data where there are legal grounds;
  • withdraw previously given consent;
  • demand the cessation of the processing of personal data in cases provided for by law;
  • appeal against the actions or inaction of the Operator to Roskomnadzor (the Russian data protection authority) or a court.

8.2. To exercise their rights, the data subject may send a request to leadyourdream@yandex.ru. Requests are considered in the manner and within the periods established by the legislation of the Russian Federation.

8.3. The withdrawal of consent to the processing of personal data by the data subject does not automatically entail the cessation of processing and the destruction of all previously provided information if the Operator has independent legal grounds for further processing.

In particular, the Operator may continue processing data to the extent necessary to fulfil obligations established by law regarding tax records, to perform existing contractual obligations, to consider claims and to protect legitimate rights and interests, in compliance with the legislation of the Russian Federation.

Processing of personal data for which there are no further legal grounds is stopped, and the data is destroyed within the periods established by law.

8.4. Submitting a lawful request to stop processing or delete personal data does not release the Operator from fulfilling obligations directly established by the legislation of the Russian Federation.
