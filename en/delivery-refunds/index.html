Receiving the e-book and refunds

Version dated November 20, 2026 · Русский

IMPORTANT: THE BOOK IS IN RUSSIAN ONLY. All language versions of the website sell the same Russian-language edition of Нескучная философия жизни. English and Spanish translations are not included.

This page explains how to receive the purchased e-book, restore download access, and contact us about technical problems or refunds.

1. What the purchase includes

The price of the e-book is RUB 399 (about $5; the final charge in your currency depends on your bank's exchange rate and fees).

One purchase includes two formats of the same Russian-language work:

  • PDF — 238 pages, excluding the cover.
  • EPUB — the same text in reflowable layout. The number of displayed pages depends on the device and reading settings.

There is no additional charge for the second format.

2. How to receive the book

After successful payment is confirmed:

  1. Two download links — PDF and EPUB — appear on the “Thank you” page.
  2. The same links are sent to the email address provided at checkout.
  3. Each link is valid for 24 hours from the moment it is created.

We recommend downloading both files right away, checking that they open, and saving them on your own device.

If the “Thank you” page did not open, check your email, including the Spam folder.

3. How to open the files

Compatible software is required to read PDF and EPUB files.

Apps for PDF: Adobe Acrobat Reader (Windows, macOS, Android, iOS), the built-in PDF viewer in Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox, Preview (macOS), Apple Books (iPhone, iPad, Mac), ReadEra (Android).

Apps for EPUB: Apple Books (iPhone, iPad, Mac), Google Play Books (Android, iOS, web — the file is uploaded to your library), ReadEra (Android), Calibre (Windows, macOS, Linux), Thorium Reader (Windows, macOS, Linux).

On iPhone, open the EPUB file via “Share” → “Books”: the Files app does not display EPUB by itself.

How to make the book look its best. The EPUB already contains its layout: alignment, indents, the poem, the table of contents. To let your reading app show it, choose the book's original formatting in the text settings (in ReadEra: “Aa” → alignment “Original”) and turn on hyphenation. Without hyphenation, many reading apps align text to the left.

Installing and configuring software on the Buyer's devices is the Buyer's own responsibility.

4. Restoring access

If the original links have expired, you can use the “Restore Access” feature.

Automatic restoration is available for seven calendar days. The period begins on the calendar day following the payment date, is determined by Moscow time, and is calculated under Articles 191–194 of the Civil Code of the Russian Federation, including extension of the final day falling on a non-business day where provided by law.

To restore access, enter:

  • the order number;
  • the email address used for the purchase.

After the order is verified, new links are sent to the same email address. Each new link is valid for 24 hours.

Automatic restoration ends after this period.

The Buyer is responsible for keeping properly received files.

This limitation does not affect the Buyer's right to make claims in case of improper performance of the agreement.

5. If a file does not open or contains an error

First, check that your app supports the selected format.

If the problem persists, email contact@leadyourdream.net.

Suggested subject: “E-book problem — order no. …”.

Describe the problem and include the order number and the email address used for the purchase, and if possible attach a screenshot of the error.

We will review the circumstances. If the problem is caused by damage to or incompleteness of the supplied file, we will send a working file free of charge or offer another lawful way to remedy the issue.

An initial response to a technical request is sent within two business days, starting on the day after it is received. This does not change the statutory deadlines for satisfying the Buyer's claims.

6. When a refund is possible

Refunds are made where there are grounds provided by law or by the Public Offer, including material breaches of the terms for supplying the purchased electronic edition.

Where both working files have been properly supplied, a change of opinion about the book's content, loss of interest in reading, or later loss of saved files do not, by themselves, constitute grounds for refunding the license fee.

This does not restrict the Buyer's rights under mandatory provisions of law.

In case of technical defects, we may offer to send a working file free of charge. The Buyer may accept this resolution by replying to our email.

7. How to request a refund

Email contact@leadyourdream.net.

Suggested subject: “Refund request — order no. …”.

State the reason for the refund and information that identifies the order. You may attach the receipt if you have it, but it is not the only acceptable proof of purchase.

Funds are normally returned through the original payment service.

If such a refund is technically impossible, we will let you know and request the necessary bank details. For convenience, we will provide a request form.

Where there are lawful grounds, the refund is made within 10 calendar days of receiving the relevant claim, unless another mandatory deadline is established by law.

For an alternative bank transfer, Section 7.8 of the Public Offer applies, including its rules on failure to provide the necessary details.

The actual crediting time also depends on banks and payment systems.
